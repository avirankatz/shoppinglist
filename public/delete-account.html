Delete your Shopping List account

Shopping List / Family Shopping List is developed by Aviran Katz. You can request deletion here without installing or signing into the app.

Email an account-deletion request
If the button does not open an email app, write to aviran.katz@gmail.com with the subject “Shopping List account deletion”.
  1. Include your Shopping List account ID if available. In the updated Android app, select “Request account deletion” in the top bar; it prepares an email containing that ID. The updated web app also has this link. An ID helps locate the account but is not by itself proof of ownership.
  2. If you cannot access the app or do not have the ID, say so. We will help identify the account and explain proportionate verification steps. Keep your existing session if possible. Never email access/refresh tokens, passwords or invite codes, and do not post requests in public GitHub issues.
  3. We process requests manually and respond without undue delay within the applicable legal deadline (normally one month under the GDPR; permitted extensions are explained). Sending the email does not immediately delete data.

What is deleted?

After verifying the request, we delete your guest authentication account, its membership records/display names, and the lists you own, including their items and memberships. Owned lists disappear for all members. List items have no author field, so content in lists owned by others cannot reliably be attributed to you; identify any personal content you want removed and we will address it while respecting others' rights.

Uninstalling or leaving a list does not delete the server account. Clear local app/site data on each device after completion. Other members' copies cannot be recalled. Backups and provider logs may persist under configured retention schedules; contact us for the current schedules. We explain any specific legal retention exception and its reason. See the privacy policy for full retention and rights details.
